Changes made here (tools/f16_convert_blender.py, tools/f16_prep_tex.py, Blender 5.2):
converted from F-16D.blend to glTF 2.0 (separate .bin + JPEG textures, because the page's security policy
blocks the blob: URLs that embedded GLB textures need); scaled from feet to metres; origin moved to the flight
model's reference centre of gravity; stabilators and rudder cut out of the fuselage, flaperons and leading-edge
flaps split left / right, all with hinge axes; duplicate tyres removed, landing gear decimated; textures
reduced from 9600 px to 2048 px; materials rebuilt as plain PBR.
