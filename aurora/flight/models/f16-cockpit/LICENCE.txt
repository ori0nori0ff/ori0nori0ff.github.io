bms-cockpit.glb and tex/2k, tex/4k are converted from the Falcon BMS 4.38 F-16CM block 50 cockpit model
(Data/TerrData/Objects/Models/888, textures of model 1052 for a few outer skin parts), (c) Benchmark Sims.
Used on this page with the site owner's permission (checked by the owner, 2026-11-14). Not covered by the licence of the
surrounding code; not to be reused elsewhere without the rights holder's permission.

Conversion (tools/build_bms_cockpit.py, deterministic): BML unpacked, switch branch 0 kept, DOFs at rest, left-handed feet ->
right-handed metres, duplicate vertices merged, quantised (KHR_mesh_quantization); textures downsized to 4096 / 2048 px JPEG / PNG,
ARMW repacked to occlusion-roughness-metalness, normal maps rebuilt from BC5, night map = backlit lettering (R) + flood light (G).

The earlier photogrammetry scan ("F-16C Cockpit Photogrammetry" by VTX, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) was removed on 2026-11-14.
The instrument face crops in tex/ keep their own note (tex/LICENCE.txt).
